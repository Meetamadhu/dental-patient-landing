Common questions

Before you book

How soon can I get an appointment?

Many new patients are seen within 48 hours. Tell us your preferred time and we’ll confirm the soonest chair that fits.

Do you accept my insurance?

We work with major PPO plans including Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, and MetLife. Bring your card—we’ll verify benefits before treatment.

Is a consult required for implants or Invisalign?

Yes—a short consult lets us route you to the right specialist, review options, and give a clear fee range before you commit.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Photo ID, insurance card (if any), and a list of medications. Arriving 10 minutes early helps us keep your chair on time.
